# Public Programme-Value Context

This page provides a transparent, **public-source estimate of programme scale**. It does **not** disclose or infer confidential contract values and it does not attribute the full commercial outcome to one engineer or one prototype.

## Public anchor

A 2016 Business Recorder report stated that the Türkiye programme covered **52 Super Mushshak aircraft for around US$50 million**.

- Source: [Business Recorder — Pakistan to supply 52 trainer aircraft to Turkey](https://www.brecorder.com/news/4454451/pakistan-to-supply-52-trainer-aircraft-to-turkey-20161124106085)
- Public benchmark: **US$50M / 52 ≈ US$0.962M per aircraft**

That figure is used only as a scale anchor. It should not be read as a catalogue price or as a statement about every later customer configuration.

Applying the Türkiye public benchmark to 80 aircraft gives an **aircraft-value scale equivalent of about US$76.9M**, rounded here to **≈US$77M**.

## Support-package sensitivity

Public reporting confirms that programme value was not limited to bare aircraft:

- Nigeria included **operational training and technical support / assistance**.
- Azerbaijan included **operational training and technical support / assistance**.
- Reporting around the Türkiye programme also recorded activity for **two Super Mushshak flight simulators** and engine repair / overhaul support arrangements.

Because public sources do not disclose a common support-package value, this repository does not invent one. Instead, the table below shows a simple sensitivity around the US$76.9M aircraft-equivalent baseline:

| Illustrative uplift for training / initial support / simulation / logistics | Campaign-scale value |
|---:|---:|
| 0% | US$76.9M |
| 10% | US$84.6M |
| 20% | US$92.3M |
| 30% | US$100.0M |

A reasonable public-facing description is therefore:

> **The later 80-aircraft export campaign represented a product-line outcome in the high tens of millions of US dollars; using a transparent public benchmark, the aircraft-equivalent scale is ≈US$77M, while an illustrative initial-support sensitivity places campaign scale at roughly US$85–100M.**

This is an **order-of-magnitude programme context**, not a summed contract disclosure, not profit, and not a claim that all later revenue was caused by the prototype work represented in this repository.

## Why this matters in an engineering portfolio

The commercial figure is secondary to the engineering evidence. Its purpose is to show the scale at which the product line later operated: a prototype and customer-evaluation programme matured into a platform family sold internationally with training, support and sustainment elements.
