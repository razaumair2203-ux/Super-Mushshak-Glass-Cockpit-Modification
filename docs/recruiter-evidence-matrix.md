## Why this repository is structured this way

A recruiter does not need dozens of internal documents. They need a **credible chain from claim to evidence**.

For that reason, the public repo shows:

- the engineering problem;
- what I owned;
- what evidence exists;
- what can safely be released;
- what remains private;
- what is uncertain.

It deliberately avoids synthetic cockpit imagery, invented interface diagrams and unsupported numerical claims.
