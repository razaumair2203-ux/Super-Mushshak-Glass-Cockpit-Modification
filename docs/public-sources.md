# Public Sources

The repository uses public sources to establish programme context independently of personal recollection.

## Government / official reporting

1. **Government of Pakistan, Ministry of Defence Production — Year Book 2017–18**  
   Reports production/overhaul activity and **glass cockpit modification of 20 Super Mushshak aircraft** for PAF / Pakistan Army. It also records Dynon SkyView glass-cockpit work and modifications for export customers.  
   https://modp.gov.pk/SiteImage/Publication/Year%20Book%202017-18%20final%20Revised.pdf

2. **Associated Press of Pakistan — Qatar contract**  
   Reports an agreement for **8 Super Mushshak aircraft** for Qatar.  
   https://www.app.com.pk/national/pakistan-inks-accord-to-supply-8-mushshak-aircraft-to-qatar/

3. **Associated Press of Pakistan — Türkiye contract**  
   Reports an agreement for **52 Super Mushshak aircraft**.  
   https://www.app.com.pk/national/pakistan-to-supply-52-trainer-aircraft-to-turkey/

4. **Associated Press of Pakistan — Azerbaijan contract**  
   Reports an agreement for **10 Super Mushshak aircraft**.  
   https://www.app.com.pk/national/pakistan-signs-agreement-with-azerbaijan-for-sale-of-10-super-mushshak-aircraft/

5. **Associated Press of Pakistan — Nigerian delivery**  
   Reports delivery under the 10-aircraft Nigerian agreement and describes the delivered trainer as equipped with a **glass cockpit** and enhanced environmental-control system.  
   https://www.app.com.pk/national/pakistan-supplies-four-super-mushshak-to-naf/

## Aerospace / historical reporting

6. **Asian Military Review — “Super Mushshaks, Super Popular!”**  
   Describes the renewed export phase after glass-cockpit integration and identifies **Garmin G950** and **Dynon** customer configurations.  
   https://www.asianmilitaryreview.com/2019/01/super-mushshaks-super-popular/

7. **Defence Journal — “Super Mushshak”**  
   Historical programme overview; discusses customer preference for Garmin 950 / Dynon SkyView glass-cockpit configurations and PAF adoption.  
   https://defencejournal.com/2018/09/10/super-mushshak/

8. **AirHistory.net — Dubai Airshow 2011 aircraft record**  
   Independently records a PAF Super Mushshak at Dubai International during the **2011 Dubai Airshow**.  
   https://www.airhistory.net/photo/722398/95-6385

9. **Garmin G900X/G950 system documentation**  
   Manufacturer documentation is used only to understand generic suite architecture. No Garmin manual pages are republished in this repository.

## Evidence discipline

Public sources establish **programme facts**. Personal photographs and archival records establish **individual participation**. The repository deliberately does not use a public programme fact to infer personal credit.
