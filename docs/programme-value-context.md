# Public programme-value context

Later commercial reporting gives context for the aircraft product line. It does not measure the retrofit team's revenue, the avionics modification's standalone value or an individual engineer's commercial contribution.

| Reported programme | Dated public report | Boundary |
|---|---|---|

| Türkiye, 52 aircraft | [Business Recorder, 24 November 2016](https://www.brecorder.com/news/4454451/pakistan-to-supply-52-trainer-aircraft-to-turkey-20161124106085): around US$50M | Contemporary report; package scope and realised revenue are not verified here |

These are different customer programmes and package definitions. No per-aircraft price is transferred to Qatar or Azerbaijan, and no percentage uplift is invented for support. The [80-aircraft four-customer sequence](public-operator-evidence.md) is a reported order-count context, not an aggregate revenue calculation.

The relevant personal contribution remains aircraft-level prototype integration, verification, troubleshooting and customer-evaluation support described in the [engineering case study](engineering-case-study.md).
