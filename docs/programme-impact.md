# Programme Impact

This page separates the **programme's documented market evolution** from the author's individual contribution.

## Early public demonstration

A Super Mushshak with an integrated glass cockpit was publicly shown at the **Dubai Airshow in November 2011**. Contemporary reporting described a two-display digital cockpit, while independent airshow records confirm Super Mushshak participation at the event.

## Later production / export configurations

Subsequent aerospace reporting describes the Super Mushshak as being offered with two glass-cockpit options:

- **Garmin G950**
- **Dynon SkyView**

Later reporting also documents a Genesys configuration; that later architecture is outside the early prototype work represented by this case study.

## Documented orders and adoption

Public sources document the following major orders during the later export phase:

| Customer | Publicly reported quantity | Context |
|---|---:|---|
| Qatar | 8 | Contract announced in 2016 |
| Nigeria | 10 | Handover reporting described glass-cockpit-equipped aircraft |
| Türkiye | 52 | Largest order in the 2016–17 export wave |
| Azerbaijan | 10 | Contract announced in 2017 |

That is **80 aircraft across four later customers** in the major 2016–17 export wave.

The Pakistan Ministry of Defence Production additionally reported **glass-cockpit modification of 20 Super Mushshak aircraft** for PAF / Pakistan Army during FY 2017–18.

## What can and cannot be inferred

It is reasonable to state that the early glass-cockpit work became part of a configuration family that later entered production, domestic upgrade and export use.

It is **not** reasonable to state that one prototype team, one engineering decision or one individual caused all subsequent aircraft sales. This repository therefore uses programme-level impact language and keeps personal-contribution claims separate.
