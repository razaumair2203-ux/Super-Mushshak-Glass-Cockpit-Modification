# Public Programme-Value Context

This page provides a transparent, **public-source estimate of programme scale**. It does **not** disclose confidential contract values and it does not attribute the full commercial outcome to one engineer or one prototype.

## Two public 2016 value anchors

Two independent public reports provide unusually consistent value anchors:

| Programme | Public value | Aircraft | Implied value / aircraft | Source |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Nigeria | US$10.2M estimated / budgeted | 10 | **US$1.020M** | [Defense News](https://www.defensenews.com/global/mideast-africa/2016/07/01/nigeria-to-acquire-super-mushshak-aircraft-from-pakistan/) |
| Türkiye | around US$50M | 52 | **US$0.962M** | [Business Recorder](https://www.brecorder.com/news/4454451/pakistan-to-supply-52-trainer-aircraft-to-turkey-20161124106085) |

These are not catalogue prices and the exact package contents differ. Their value is that they independently place a 2016 Super Mushshak export aircraft at roughly **US$0.96M–1.02M per aircraft**.

A simple weighted public benchmark across the 62 aircraft covered by those two reports is:

**(US$50M + US$10.2M) / (52 + 10) ≈ US$0.971M per aircraft**

## Publicly reported 2016–2017 new-customer sequence

| Customer | Aircraft | Public source |
|---|---:|---|
| Qatar | 8 | [Associated Press of Pakistan](https://www.app.com.pk/national/pakistan-inks-accord-to-supply-8-mushshak-aircraft-to-qatar/) |
| Nigeria | 10 | [Associated Press of Pakistan](https://www.app.com.pk/national/pakistan-supplies-four-super-mushshak-to-naf/) |
| Türkiye | 52 | [Associated Press of Pakistan](https://www.app.com.pk/national/pakistan-to-supply-52-trainer-aircraft-to-turkey/) |
| Azerbaijan | 10 | [Associated Press of Pakistan](https://www.app.com.pk/national/pakistan-signs-agreement-with-azerbaijan-for-sale-of-10-super-mushshak-aircraft/) |
| **Total** | **80** | public customer totals above |

Applying the two public unit-value anchors to an 80-aircraft campaign gives:

- Türkiye anchor: **80 × US$0.962M ≈ US$76.9M**
- Nigeria anchor: **80 × US$1.020M ≈ US$81.6M**
- weighted two-anchor benchmark: **80 × US$0.971M ≈ US$77.7M**

The repository therefore uses **≈US$78M** as a conservative aircraft-value scale benchmark.

## Support-package sensitivity

Public reporting confirms that programme value was not limited to aircraft:

- Nigeria included **operational training and technical support / assistance**.
- Azerbaijan included **operational training and technical support / assistance**.
- Reporting around the Türkiye programme recorded activity around **two Super Mushshak flight simulators** and engine repair / overhaul support arrangements.

The public sources do not disclose a common support-package value, so this repository does not present a fabricated package total. Instead, the table below gives an explicit sensitivity around the US$77.7M weighted aircraft benchmark:

| Illustrative uplift for training / initial support / simulation / logistics | Campaign-scale value |
|---:|---:|
| 0% | US$77.7M |
| 10% | US$85.4M |
| 20% | US$93.2M |
| 30% | US$101.0M |

A concise public-facing description is therefore:

> **The later 80-aircraft export campaign represented a product-line outcome in the high tens of millions of US dollars. Two independent 2016 public value anchors imply an aircraft-equivalent scale of approximately US$77–82M; an illustrative initial-support sensitivity places campaign scale at roughly US$85–100M.**

This is an **order-of-magnitude programme context**, not a summed contract disclosure, not profit, and not a claim that all later revenue was caused by the prototype work represented in this repository.

## Why this belongs in the portfolio

The commercial figure is secondary to the engineering evidence. Its purpose is to show the scale at which the product line later operated: a prototype and customer-evaluation programme matured into an internationally fielded aircraft family with training, support and sustainment elements.
