## Evidence-strength definitions

**Direct** — record created during the programme that directly demonstrates the activity, configuration or authorship claimed.

**Corroborating** — an additional project record consistent with a direct claim but not, by itself, the primary proof.

**Public** — external open-source material suitable for programme-level context but not used to establish private individual-role claims.

## Sanitization standard

The public repo excludes:

- security/classification markings;
- organization logos and internal stationery;
- internal document/reference numbers;
- personal telephone numbers and private email addresses;
- precise project/unit locations;
- controlled wiring, pinout or installation detail;
- confidential vendor manuals, pricing or proprietary diagrams;
- private customer correspondence.

Photographs are limited to crop, exposure/quality adjustment and local redaction of identifying marks. **No synthetic aircraft or cockpit content is generated.**

## Known uncertainty: Garmin nomenclature

The surviving archive uses G950/G900-family terminology in several formal records and also contains G3X comparative/prototype references.

Therefore the repo uses **Garmin G900/G950-family track** as a conservative umbrella description. It does not retrospectively assert an exact flown sub-variant where the surviving evidence is inconsistent.

This is intentional evidence discipline, not a missing-data problem to be filled by inference.
