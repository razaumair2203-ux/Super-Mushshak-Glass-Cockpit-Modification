# Independent Public Evidence

Project material documents the **engineering work**. The sources below independently document the wider product outcome: public appearance, export contracts, operator service and later avionics evolution.

## What independent reporting establishes

| Source | Supported point |
|---|---|
| [Times Aerospace — 2017](https://www.timesaerospace.aero/features/defence/nigeria-opts-for-super-mushshaks) | a two-display glass-cockpit Super Mushshak first appeared publicly at Dubai Airshow 2011; Dynon and Garmin versions were offered |
| [Asian Military Review — 2019](https://www.asianmilitaryreview.com/2019/01/super-mushshaks-super-popular/) | 2016–2017 new-customer sequence totalled 80 aircraft: Qatar 8, Nigeria 10, Türkiye 52, Azerbaijan 10; Qatar/Türkiye Garmin 950, Nigeria Dynon |
| [Times Aerospace — 2024](https://www.timesaerospace.aero/sites/aerospace/times/files/magazines/2024/das24-wds-d3/content/das24-wds-d3.pdf) | more than 100 Super Mushshaks had reportedly been upgraded or sold with Dynon, GenesyS or Garmin avionics since 2016 |
| [Defence Journal](https://www.defencejournal.com/2018/09/10/super-mushshak/) | overseas users are described as preferring Garmin 950 or Dynon SkyView; PAF Dynon adoption is also described |

## Export / operator cross-check

| Customer / operator | Quantity | Independent / official evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| **Nigeria** | 10 | [APP delivery report](https://www.app.com.pk/national/pakistan-supplies-four-super-mushshak-to-naf/) states delivered aircraft were glass-cockpit equipped; [Nigerian Air Force](https://airforce.mil.ng/news/pakistan-to-strengthen-technical%2C-defence-cooperation-with-nigerian-air-force361186473) confirms acquisition of 10 |
| **Qatar** | 8 | [APP contract report](https://www.app.com.pk/national/pakistan-inks-accord-to-supply-8-mushshak-aircraft-to-qatar/); [Qatar News Agency](https://qna.org.qa/en/news/news-details?date=23%2F01%2F2024&id=0033-hh%C2%A0the-amir-patronizes%C2%A0graduation-ceremony-of%C2%A0al-zaeem-air-academy) documents continuing Super Mushshak use at Al Zaeem Air Academy |
| **Türkiye** | 52 | [APP contract report](https://www.app.com.pk/national/pakistan-to-supply-52-trainer-aircraft-to-turkey/); [Türkiye Ministry of National Defence](https://www.msb.gov.tr/SlaytHaber/1d609ba0df7c40039347230972cec89b) records aircraft entering Turkish Air Force inventory |
| **Azerbaijan** | 10 | [APP report](https://www.app.com.pk/national/pakistan-signs-agreement-with-azerbaijan-for-sale-of-10-super-mushshak-aircraft/) quoting the programme sale announcement |
| **2016–2017 total** | **80** | independently summarized by Asian Military Review |

The defensible programme-level conclusion is that the glass-cockpit development path represented in this repository became part of a **successful multi-country Super Mushshak product line**.

Later production and export configurations evolved, so configuration-specific technical claims in this repository remain tied to the relevant prototype / test configuration.

## Relation to this portfolio

The role represented here is **Lead Systems Engineer** for the retrofit: aircraft-level architecture/integration, prototype ground and flight test, troubleshooting, OEM technical coordination and international customer-evaluation support.
